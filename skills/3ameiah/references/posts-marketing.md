# Posts, Social, and Marketing

Use this playbook for single posts, threads, carousels, captions, ads, landing pages, emails, launch assets, offers, and coordinated campaigns.

## Establish the contract

1. Identify the artifact and channel: post, thread, carousel, caption, ad, landing page, email, or campaign bundle.
2. Identify audience stage, objective, central idea, desired action, platform, format, length, and brand voice.
3. Lock the selected mode and whether the source is meaning-preserving, source-locked, or creatively adaptable.
4. Inventory protected material: exact quotes, handles, links, hashtags, slide count, visual premise, offer terms, proof, prices, dates, and CTA destination.
5. If this sells anything, build an evidence map before drafting.

## Evidence map

For each proposed claim, mark it as verified, supplied-but-unverified, illustrative, or unsupported:

- Product features and deliverables.
- Price, discount, payment terms, and eligibility.
- Deadline, scarcity, seat count, urgency, and availability.
- Testimonials, social proof, statistics, and performance results.
- Guarantee, refund terms, outcome, transformation, and risk language.

Use only verified or explicitly supplied claims. Do not polish unsupported claims into asserted facts. Keep uncertainty and conditions visible.

## Build the artifact

Apply the selected mode at structural level. In `3ameiah-friendly`, use warmer framing, natural address, useful transitions, and an easy conversational cadence; do not deliver ordinary `3ameiah` with one pet phrase or emoji added.

### Single post or caption

- Open with a real tension, observation, benefit, question, or visual premise from the brief.
- Deliver a concrete idea or takeaway; slang is not value.
- Keep paragraph rhythm and line breaks appropriate to the channel.
- Add a CTA only when the objective calls for one, and make it specific and proportionate.

### Thread

- Give each part one job and maintain a clear progression.
- Preserve numbering and links; avoid restating the same hook in every entry.
- Make the final action consistent with the opening promise.

### Carousel

- Respect requested slide count and visual premise.
- Give every slide a single clear beat with enough context to stand alone.
- Keep title/body hierarchy concise and synchronize the caption with slide claims.
- Do not invent filler slides to hit a count. Combine or deepen supported value instead.

### Landing page, ad, or campaign bundle

- Map message to funnel stage and CTA destination.
- Keep offer, price, proof, eligibility, deadline, guarantee, and disclaimer consistent across every asset.
- Preserve functional routes, form behavior, tracking, and legal/disclaimer copy.
- For a code implementation, also read [product-ui.md](product-ui.md).

## Validate the result

- Compare every factual and commercial claim against the evidence map.
- Check that hook, delivered value, and CTA describe the same promise.
- Check quote fidelity, handles, URLs, campaign codes, slide count, caption-carousel agreement, and cross-asset consistency.
- Read aloud for mode naturalness and remove hype, filler, repeated discourse markers, and literal English idioms.
- Verify current platform limits only when they materially affect delivery; platform rules drift, so do not rely on hard-coded limits in this skill.
- Preview/render the real asset when layout, truncation, or direction matters.

## Edge handling

- A request to “make it sell harder” does not authorize false scarcity, fake proof, stronger guarantees, or invented outcomes.
- Preserve a supplied central visual or story premise; do not replace it with a generic marketing template.
- Clearly separate exact customer quotes from rewritten paraphrases.
- If source evidence is missing, write around supported benefits or request the missing fact; never fill the gap with confidence.
